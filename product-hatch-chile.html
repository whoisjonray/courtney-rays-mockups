What it's like

A Sweet Slow Burn.

Real Hatch chiles. Not "spicy seasoning." We roast them, grind them, and fold them into the brittle while it's still hot.

🍯
First bite: sweet

You taste pecan, butter, and caramel. The chiles are quiet. You think it might not be spicy at all. Reader: it is.

🌶️
Mid-chew: warmth

The Hatch chiles arrive about 4 seconds in. Not hot — warm. The kind of heat that opens up the pecan flavor, not bury it.

Common questions

FAQ

How spicy is it on a scale of 1–10? +
Around a 4. Mild-medium. The sweetness blunts most of the heat. Kids who like buffalo wing flavor handle it fine. Spice-sensitive folks should start with one piece.
Why Hatch chiles specifically? +
Hatch chiles (grown only in the Hatch Valley, NM) have a distinct sweet-roasted flavor that pairs naturally with caramel and pecan. We've tried jalapeño, ghost, serrano. None of them work like Hatch does.
Is it really vegan? +
Yes. Hatch Chile Pecan uses our plant-based butter. Same fat, no dairy. Gluten-free, vegan, and free of high-fructose corn syrup.
Why does it sometimes go out of stock? +
Hatch chile harvest is August–September. We buy and freeze enough for the year. Some years we run out by spring. We're working on increasing the buy.
