Common questions

FAQ

Is this gluten free? +
Yes. All our brittle is naturally gluten-free. We don't use wheat, barley, or rye in the kitchen.
It says vegan. But isn't there butter? +
Good question. Our classic peanut brittle uses a plant-based butter alternative — same fat ratio, same buttery flavor, no dairy. Bacon Pecan and the freeze-dried line have their own ingredient profiles; check each bag.
Why is it more expensive than store brittle? +
Real ingredients cost more. Real butter alternatives cost more. Hand-cooking in small batches takes longer than running a candy line. We're not the cheapest. We're trying to be the best.
How fresh is it when it arrives? +
We cook on Tuesdays and Thursdays, ship Wednesday and Friday. Nothing sits longer than 7 days before it ships. Most orders arrive within 4–5 days of being made.
