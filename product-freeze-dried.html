Common questions

FAQ

What does freeze drying do? +
It pulls the moisture out of the brittle while keeping the flavor compounds intact. The result is a lighter, airier texture that dissolves on your tongue — but tastes more concentrated than the original.
Is this gluten-free and vegan? +
Yes. The freeze dried line is fully plant-based, dairy-free, and gluten-free. We swap butter for a neutral oil in this recipe so the freeze dryer works cleanly.
How long does it stay fresh? +
About 6 months in the bag, sealed. Once you open it, eat within 2 weeks for best texture. (You won't make it that long. Trust.)
Does it ship internationally? +
US + Canada only right now. We're working on EU shipping for late 2026.
Why is it sometimes sold out? +
Freeze drying takes 24 hours per batch. We make limited runs. When it's gone, it's gone for ~10 days.
